This folder is where your actual audio files go.

I can't include real downloadable songs (copyright), so you need
to add your own mp3 files here, named exactly:

  song1.mp3
  song2.mp3
  song3.mp3

Where to get audio you're allowed to use:
- Your own recordings
- Royalty-free sites like Pixabay Music, Free Music Archive, or
  YouTube Audio Library (check the license on each track)
- Sample loops/jingles that come with music software you own

Once the files are in this folder, PinkTunes will play them
automatically - the app already points at /music/song1.mp3, etc.
See the README.md in the project root for more details.
